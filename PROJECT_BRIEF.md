# Lengura Reader — personal project brief

## Goal
Build Ricky a local German-learning desk that combines Lengura videos with transcript study and directly supports his Goethe B1 plan.

## Current experience
- Browse/search 15 Lengura lessons labeled A2–B2.
- Every lesson has a deep link that selects its YouTube embed in the reader and provides a direct link to the official Lengura transcript page.
- The “Life in Spain” conversation has a local 191-turn transcript with draft English, search, translation toggle, line navigation, learned marks, and personal Franklin notes.

## Transcript boundary
- The 14 other full transcripts stay on Lengura and are linked, not copied into this repository.
- For additional local transcripts, use material Ricky owns or has permission to redistribute, or add concise original study notes instead.
- Keep machine-translated English clearly labeled until reviewed.

## Current limits
- One transcript is locally included; 14 are linked to source pages.
- Transcript lines have no timestamps and do not seek within the video.
- YouTube/Lengura links require internet; a video may disallow embedding and need the YouTube fallback.
