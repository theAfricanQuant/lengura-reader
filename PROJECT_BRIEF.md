# Lengura Reader — personal project brief

## Goal
Build Ricky a local German-learning desk that combines Lengura videos with transcript study and directly supports his Goethe B1 plan.

## Current experience
- Browse and search 15 candidate Lengura lessons labeled A2, B1, or B2.
- Watch the B1–B2 Milica/Henry conversation beside its complete 191-turn source transcript.
- Toggle English, search the text, move between turns, mark learned lines, and keep personal Franklin-method notes in browser storage.
- Jump from the reader to the existing Franklin workbook, 50-pattern drill, and B1 roadmap.

## Completion expectations
- For every lesson brought into the local reader: full transcript, speaker labels, sentence-aligned English, transparent source and level, responsive layout.
- Mark English machine translations clearly until proofread; keep transcript gaps visible.
- Expand lesson coverage only after verifying transcript completeness and translation quality.

## Current limitations
- Only one of 15 candidate lessons has a local full reader.
- English beyond the opening 18 turns is a machine-translation draft, not reviewed.
- Transcript turns have no timestamps and do not seek the video.
- Requires internet for YouTube and Lengura source links.
